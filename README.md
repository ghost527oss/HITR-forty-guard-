# HITR — Heat Intelligence & Territorial Resilience

FortyGuard Hackathon'26 · Track 01 (Resilient Cities & Infrastructure) + Track 06 (Agentic AI)

**HITR turns a heat map into a ranked, location-specific action plan** for cities, farmers, and
communities: where to plant trees, add shelter-belts and shade structures, place water stations, and
orient buildings to cool homes, protect vulnerable people, and keep farmland productive — powered by
FortyGuard's hyperlocal Temperature API.

## Principles
- Plan **interventions on existing cities** — never rebuild from scratch.
- Heat is one of several **livability** factors (accessibility, equity, productivity).
- The program does the real analysis; the AI is a bounded, grounded assistant.
- Works for **any city**.
- Everything is **versioned and documented**.
